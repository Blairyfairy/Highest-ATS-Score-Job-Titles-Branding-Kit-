# Identity rules

Primary: original lavender gemstone + Blair Page wordmark. Secondary: portrait inside one perfectly circular alpha mask. Do not stretch, skew, or re-crop. Minimum portrait: 64px. Minimum logo clear space: 1/4 of symbol height. Use dark background for pale wordmark. Uploaded raster gemstone is the authoritative icon; SVG wordmark is an editable approximation, not a vector trace of the gemstone.
